# CacheOut — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 August 2026**

## The short version

CacheOut collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. What you
write stays on your device, and syncs — if you want it to — through your own iCloud, where only you
can read it.

That is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. It is a description of an app that
never receives any.

## What CacheOut stores, and where

Everything you type, dictate, scribble or scan is stored **on your device**. That includes the line
itself, anything you struck through, and the original recording, drawing or photo behind a line.

If you are signed in to iCloud, that data also syncs to **your own private iCloud database**, so it
appears on your other devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database. The developer of CacheOut
has no access to it, cannot read it, and receives no copy of it — Apple's infrastructure holds it on
your behalf, under your Apple Account, subject to
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you are not signed in to iCloud, CacheOut works exactly the same, entirely offline. Nothing leaves
the device at all.

## What CacheOut sends to the developer

Nothing.

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, no third-party code
of any kind in the app. There is no server operated by the developer for CacheOut to talk to, so there
is no place for your data to be sent even in principle.

## Permissions, and why they are asked for

Each of these is requested only at the moment you tap the control that needs it, and declining any of
them leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

- **Microphone and Speech Recognition** — so you can dictate a line instead of typing it. Transcription
  runs on your device. The audio is stored with the scrap, on your device, and is never uploaded to the
  developer.
- **Camera** — so you can photograph a note on paper and have the text read out of it. The image is
  processed on your device and stored with the scrap.

## Artificial intelligence

CacheOut's suggestions — what kind of thing a line might be, what topics it mentions — are produced
**on your device**, using Apple's on-device models. Your text is not sent to the developer, to OpenAI,
or to any other service for processing. On devices without the necessary hardware the app falls back to
a small set of rules that also runs locally, and if neither is available it simply makes no
suggestions.

## Children

CacheOut is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Because the
app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
