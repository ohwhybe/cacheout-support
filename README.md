# CacheOut — Support

Digital scrap paper for iPhone. Open it, type the thing, strike it when it's done.

**This repository is for support and privacy documents only. It is not the app's source code.**

## Getting help

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/ohwhybe/cacheout-support/issues)** — bug reports, questions,
feature suggestions, all of it. That is the only support channel, and it is checked.

If you are reporting something that went wrong, these three things save a round trip:

1. What you did, and what happened instead.
2. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
3. Whether it happens every time, or happened once.

Issues are public. **Don't paste anything private** — the text of a scrap, a screenshot with your real
list in it. A description is almost always enough, and if it isn't, say so and we'll find another way.

## Privacy

[Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

The short version: CacheOut collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. What
you write stays on your device and syncs, if you want it to, through your own iCloud — where only you
can read it.

## Frequently asked

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start writing.

**Is there a subscription?**
No. You buy CacheOut once. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no free tier to be
nudged out of. There's no revenue in your attention, so there's nothing here designed to bring you
back more often than you need it.

**Does it sync between my devices?**
Yes, through your own private iCloud, if you're signed in. If you're not, everything still works — it
just stays on the one device.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Capture never waits for a network. Anything you write offline syncs when you're back on.

**Where did my completed items go?**
Into the Archive, grouped by the day you struck them — the toolbar icon at the top right. Nothing is
ever deleted, and one tap brings anything back.

**Something I never finished disappeared.**
It didn't. Anything you capture and don't strike comes back on its own after a week, in a section near
the bottom of the list. You never have to set a date for that to happen.

**Do I have to categorise things?**
No, and that's rather the point. The app will quietly suggest what a line might be, and you can ignore
it forever with nothing degrading.

**Why don't I see any AI suggestions?**
On-device intelligence needs recent hardware. Without it the app uses a simpler local rule set, and if
that has no opinion it stays quiet. Everything else works identically — classification is optional
everywhere in the app.
